## Why this was deprecated

The legacy spec gave concrete column structures and example formulas keyed to SaaS subscription economics. While the **discipline** of logging every estimate's calculation generalizes well, the **template** doesn't. v2.0 moves the discipline into Phase D of the interview guide and lets the calculation-log columns adapt to the methodology the user and SCOPER agree on.
