## Why this was deprecated

In SaaS the 회사명 ≠ 서비스명 distinction matters (e.g., "91APP Inc." owns "91APP" the SaaS product). In digital advertising the relevant disambiguation is **advertiser ↔ agency ↔ media platform**, three columns not two. In fintech it's **issuer ↔ network ↔ acquirer**. The right notation is methodology-derived, not skill-prescribed.
